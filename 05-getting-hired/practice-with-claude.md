# Practising with Claude

You don't need a friend free every evening to practise customer calls and interviews. Paste one of these prompts into Claude (claude.ai, or Claude Code in this repo) and do the exercise **out loud or typed live**, without notes. Keep the transcripts in `05-getting-hired/answers/`.

> The [Field Console](../console/README.md) now runs prompts 1 and 3 for you, with hidden facts, a talk-time meter and a scored debrief: **Stakeholder calls** and **Inject cards**. Use the prompts below when you want a character the Console doesn't have, or to practise outside the app.

## 1. Sceptical customer: discovery call
> Role-play the sponsor in `04-engagements/01-lakeside-clinics.md` (the CEO). Stay in character. You want a 24/7 AI in 6 weeks and you're impatient with questions. Only reveal facts from the memos if I ask the right questions. Push back if I propose solutions too early. After I say "END CALL", step out of character and score me 1–5 on: problem discovery, quantified success metric, stakeholder mapping, constraint discovery, and clear next steps. Quote the moments that cost me points.

## 2. Decomposition interviewer
> You are an FDE interviewer at an AI company. Give me one decomposition question from `05-getting-hired/interview-bank.md` (or a harder one of your own). Answer my clarifying questions with realistic, sometimes inconvenient facts. After 30 minutes or when I say "DONE", give hiring-committee-style feedback: strong hire / hire / no hire, with reasons.

## 3. Angry exec escalation
> You are the COO of Savanna Microfinance. The copilot told a loan officer the wrong maximum loan size and a loan was approved in breach of policy. You've just found out and you're angry. I'm the FDE on a call with you. Be realistic, not cartoonish. After the call, tell me what built trust and what lost it.

## 4. Code review of my lab work
> Review `02-technical-depth/<module>/lab` like a senior FDE whose customer will run this in production next week. Focus on what would hurt the customer: idempotency, failure handling, security, observability, and whether the docs are enough for someone else to run it. Don't rewrite it. Give me a ranked list.

## 5. Exec explanation drill
> I'll explain <topic> as if to a non-technical CFO in under 90 seconds. Then you play the CFO and ask the two questions a CFO would actually ask. Then tell me which sentence of mine was jargon.

**Rule:** the AI plays the customer or the interviewer. It never does the exercise for you. The point is reps under pressure.
