# 06 — Beyond: careers and businesses built on FDE skills

FDE is a starting point as well as a goal. The same skills (finding the real problem, shipping into hostile environments, measuring outcomes) lead to several roles and to building your own business.

## Roles once you've mastered this

| Role | What it is | Typical next step from |
|---|---|---|
| **Forward Deployed Engineer** | Build and ship inside the customer's environment | This curriculum |
| **Applied AI Engineer** (AI labs) | FDE work with more focus on evals and feeding learnings back to research and product | FDE + strong eval portfolio |
| **AI Solutions Architect / Solutions Engineer** | Design the system and help win the deal; build less | FDE, if you enjoy the pre-sale side |
| **Founding engineer** at an AI startup | At early startups the first engineers are the deployment team | FDE at a startup |
| **FDE lead → Head of Deployment / Field Engineering** | Run a team of FDEs; turn repeated workarounds into reusable kits | 2–3 years as FDE |
| **Product manager (technical)** | Decide what gets built, based on patterns across customers | FDE who writes great field feedback |
| **Fractional Head of AI / AI consultant** | Part-time AI lead for several mid-sized companies | FDE + references |
| **Founder** | Turn the problem you keep solving into a product or firm | Any of the above |

## Webfront360: possible directions

*Based on the portfolio description: AI-generated vertical video for local businesses (Remotion), scheduling, performance learning. Correct this where it's wrong.*

| # | Direction | What changes | FDE skills it uses | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Multi-location brands** (pharmacy chains, restaurant groups, banks, telco franchises) | Head-office approval flows, per-branch content, SSO, brand-safety checks on generated content | 02/02, 02/03 | Longer sales cycles; much bigger contracts; hard for small competitors to copy |
| 2 | **Prove the videos sell** | Link content performance to POS / Mobile Money sales: closed-loop attribution | 02/01, plus EatryCloud data | Needs data access from customers; becomes the reason they can't leave |
| 3 | **Growth suite**: Webfront360 + RunMySales + EatryCloud | Content brings customers in → WhatsApp agent converts → POS records the sale → learning loop | All of it | Three products to maintain; the strongest story if focused on one vertical (e.g. restaurants) |
| 4 | **Forward-deployed AI services firm** | Fixed-fee discovery sprint → deployment → monthly run retainer; your products are the toolkit. Later: become an AWS Partner and build a credentialed partner-led FDE practice ([AWS FDE track](../aws-fde-track/README.md)) | 03, 04 | Services revenue funds the product, but services can swallow product time |
| 5 | **White-label for marketing agencies** | Agencies resell and deploy; you supply the platform | 02/02 (multi-tenant) | Scales without you on every account, but you capture less value and learn less from end users |

**Recommendation:** 2 + 3 focused on one vertical (restaurants, where EatryCloud already gives you the data), funded by 4 if you go independent. Being able to say how much a video actually sold is the hardest part for competitors to copy. Decide early whether this runs **alongside** an FDE job (do a narrow version of 2) or **instead** of one (4 → 3).

## Exercise: validate before you build

Use the customer-craft skills on your own business:
1. Run discovery calls ([template](../03-customer-craft/templates/discovery-call.md)) with **10** potential customers for your chosen direction. No pitching. Ask about the problem and what they spend on it today.
2. Write `06-beyond/webfront360-findings.md`: what people actually said, how many would pay, how much, and what surprised you.
3. Write a [business case](../03-customer-craft/templates/business-case.md) for one customer, using their numbers.
4. Only then write the roadmap. If fewer than 3 of the 10 would pay for a pilot, the direction is wrong. Pick another and repeat.
