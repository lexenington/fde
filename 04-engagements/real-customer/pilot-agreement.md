# Pilot agreement (template, one page)

> Plain-language template, not legal advice. For anything involving money, personal data or regulated sectors, have a lawyer or your sponsor's legal team read it. Send it **before** you see any real data, and keep the signed copy (a photo or a reply email that says "agreed" is enough for a first pilot).

**Between:** [Customer organisation] ("the Customer") and Alexander Agboada ("I")
**Date:** ____

**1. What we are trying to do**
[One sentence: the problem, and the measurable result. For example "Cut the time to log a delivery note from 12 minutes to under 3, measured over two weeks."]

**2. What I will do**
Build and run [the solution] for [3-5] weeks, starting ____. I will report progress weekly in writing. I will hand over a runbook so your team can run it without me.

**3. What the Customer will provide**
Access to [named documents, messages, systems], about [20 minutes a week] of [named person]'s time, and a person who can say yes to changes.

**4. Data**
I will only use the data named in section 3, only for this pilot, and only on [devices or accounts agreed]. I will not share it with anyone else or use it for another customer. Where an AI service processes it, I will tell you which one and what it keeps. At the end I will delete my copies and tell you in writing. Personal data is handled under the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012.

**5. Access**
I will ask for the least access I need, in writing, and give it back when the pilot ends.

**6. What I may say about it afterwards**
I may write a case study about the approach and the results. I will show you the draft first. You choose: ☐ named ☐ anonymised ☐ not at all. I may ask you for a short quote and a reference if you are happy with the result.

**7. Ending it**
Either of us can stop at any time by telling the other. Nothing is owed by either side.

**8. Who owns what**
You own your data and the outputs built from it. I keep ownership of my general tools and know-how. If you want to continue after the pilot, we will agree terms in writing first.

Signed: ____ (Customer) ____ (Alexander)
