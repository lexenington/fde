# Finding the real customer: outreach tracker

Engagement 3 is the one that gives you something no simulation can: **a real person who says "they solved our problem."** It has the longest lead time in the whole plan, so the sending starts in **week 2** and never stops until you have a yes. Expect to message 20-30 people to get 3 conversations and 1 customer.

**Targets**: week 2, 5 sent. Week 3, 5 more and a conversation booked. Week 4, a **discovery call held**. Week 5, scoping doc sent. Week 6, baseline measured. If you're behind these numbers, send more messages, not better ones.

## Who to ask

Start with people who already trust you: clients from your freelance years, restaurants on EatryCloud, anyone you've shipped something for. Then friends-of-friends: ask each of the first group *"who do you know who loses hours to paperwork or WhatsApp?"*

**Good candidates** have: a painful manual process that repeats daily, a person who owns it, documents or messages you can look at, and a number you can measure (hours, errors, response time). Document processing, WhatsApp order-status or support, Mobile Money reconciliation, an internal policy assistant. Reuse your labs.

**Avoid**: anything medical or financial where a mistake harms someone and nobody senior will take responsibility; anything where you can't get the real data; anyone who wants to pay you to build "an AI" with no problem attached.

## Message drafts (edit them; make them sound like you)

**WhatsApp or SMS, someone you know**
> Hi [Name], it's Alexander. I'm putting together case studies on using AI to remove repetitive admin for businesses in Ghana. I'd like to spend 3 weeks, free, solving one painful manual process for someone. I thought of you because [specific reason: "you told me your team retypes every delivery note"]. Could I take 20 minutes this week to see how it works today? No pitch, I mostly want to watch.

**Email, someone you don't know well**
> Subject: 20 minutes to look at one process in [organisation]?
>
> Hello [Name], I'm a software engineer in Accra who builds AI agents that handle things like WhatsApp orders, invoice processing and bookings. I'm building case studies and would like to spend three weeks, unpaid, fixing one real bottleneck for one organisation, in return for being allowed to write it up (anonymised if you prefer) and to ask you for a reference if it works.
> I'd start by watching how [process] works today for 20 minutes. If it isn't a good fit, we both lose nothing. Would [day] or [day] suit you?

**Follow-up, no reply after 4 days (once)**
> Hi [Name], a quick nudge in case this got buried. Even five minutes on the phone to tell me "not now" helps me. Thank you.

**If they ask the price**
> It's free for this first one. In exchange I'd ask for 20 minutes a week of your time or your team's, access to the real documents or messages (under a written agreement, see the pilot agreement), and permission to write it up.

## Before the first call

- Read the [discovery-call template](../../03-customer-craft/templates/discovery-call.md). Don't decide the solution before the call.
- Practise first with the Console's stakeholder calls (Nana, then Samuel). The real call is the same skill with real stakes.
- Ask to **see** the work: a screen share, the spreadsheet, the actual WhatsApp group.
- Agree the next step with a date before you hang up.

## After it works

Get a **written quote** from the sponsor, and sign-off on the case study, anonymised or not. See [`03-real-customer.md`](../03-real-customer.md) for the rules (baseline before you build, ship to real users, measure for two weeks).
