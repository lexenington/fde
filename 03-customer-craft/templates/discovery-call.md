# Discovery call: <Customer> / <date>

**Attendees:** (name, role, and which one: buyer / user / blocker / champion)
**Goal of this call:** understand the problem well enough to write a scoping doc tomorrow.

## Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for the time. I'd like to understand how this works today before we talk about any solution. Is it OK if I ask a lot of questions and take notes?"

## The problem (20 min)
- What prompted you to look at this *now*? What happens if you do nothing for 6 months?
- Walk me through the last time this happened, step by step. Who did what, in which tool?
- How often does it happen? (per day/week, and how do you know?)
- What does it cost you: time, money, customers, risk? Has anyone put a number on it?
- What have you tried already? Why didn't it work?
- Can you show me? (screen share the spreadsheet, the inbox, the system)

## Success (10 min)
- If this worked perfectly, what would be different in 3 months? What would you measure?
- What's the number today? (baseline, and if nobody knows, getting it is step 1)
- What would make you say this project failed?

## Constraints & people (10 min)
- Where does the data live? Who owns each system? Who can grant access?
- Who else needs to say yes? (IT, security, legal, finance, union/works council)
- Any compliance requirements? (data protection, data residency, audit)
- Timeline drivers: a board meeting, a budget cycle, peak season?
- Who will run this day-to-day after we're done?

## Close (3 min)
- "Here's what I heard: <problem>, measured by <metric>, currently <baseline>. Did I get that right?"
- "Next step: I'll send a one-page scope by <date>. Can we review it on <date>?"
