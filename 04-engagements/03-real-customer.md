# Engagement 3 — A real customer

Simulations teach the moves. A real customer teaches the job, and gives you something no simulation can: **a reference who will say "they solved our problem."**

## Finding one
You've freelanced in Ghana since 2016. Go back to that network: a school, a pharmacy chain, a logistics company, a church organisation, a restaurant group already on EatryCloud. Pitch:

> "I'm building case studies in AI automation. I'd like to spend 3 weeks, free, solving one painful manual process for you. In exchange, I'd like to write it up (anonymised if you prefer) and use you as a reference."

## Rules
- Run real discovery ([template](../03-customer-craft/templates/discovery-call.md)). Don't decide the solution before the call.
- The scope must have a **baseline metric measured before you build**.
- Ship to production with real users, even if it's only 5 of them.
- Measure for at least 2 weeks after go-live.
- Get a written quote from the sponsor at the end.

## Good candidate problems
- Document processing (admissions forms, delivery notes, invoices). Reuse 02/03.
- WhatsApp customer support / order status. Reuse RunMySales.
- Reconciling Mobile Money statements against sales records. Reuse 02/01 + your Paystack experience.
- An internal knowledge assistant over the organisation's policies.
